## 동정 포인트(요청 항목 하나씩 확인)
1. 청회색 윗면 — 위키 "Adults are slate-grey above with a dark crown"; BOW "steel-blue upperparts". (NIBR은 "몸 윗면은 회갈색"이라고 적음 — 어린새·광선에 따른 차이로 보이며 성조 묘사는 위키·BOW를 따름.)
2. 길고 가는 뾰족한 날개 — 위키 "appearing sickle-like in flight with its long pointed wings and square tail"; Hobby (bird) 위키 "a fairly small, very swift falcon with long, narrow wings" https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hobby_(bird) ; RSPB "longer, sharply pointed wings".
3. 비교적 짧은 꼬리 — RSPB "They also have a shorter tail than Kestrels" https://www.rspb.org.uk/birds-and-wildlife/hobby . 단, HKBWS "Long-winged falcon with pointed wing tips and moderately long tail" https://avifauna.hkbws.org.hk/species/0220/029900 → 황조롱이와 견준 상대 특징으로만 확인.
4. 가슴·배의 굵은 검은 세로줄 — BOW "bold dark streaks on the breast and belly over a whitish background" https://birdsoftheworld.org/bow/species/eurhob/cur/introduction ; HKBWS "broad dark streaks on underparts"; 위키 "whitish with black streaks".
5. 적갈색 허벅지·아래꼬리덮깃 — 위키 "the thighs and undertail coverts are unstreaked rufous ... the red 'trousers' and vent"; NIBR "아랫배와 아래꼬리덮깃은 붉은색"; BOW "red or reddish thighs and undertail coverts".
6. 검은 뺨선과 흰 뺨·멱 — 위키 "two short black moustache stripes. The throat is unstreaked white"; BOW "a prominent black moustachial stripe that contrasts against the white cheek"; RSPB "white cheeks with a black 'moustache' and eye mask"; HKBWS "white throat and half-collar, blackish and tapered moustachial stripe".
- 맨살 부위: RSPB "Dark eyes with a yellow rim", "Black beak with yellow at the base", "Yellow legs".

## 암수·어린새
- 위키 "Sexes are similar."
- 위키 "Juveniles are generally much browner, with scaled upper parts and streaked buffy thighs and undertail coverts." RSPB "Juveniles are browner and lack the red 'trousers'". HKBWS "browner above and below, has pale fringes to mantle, scapulars and coverts ... paler rufous on the underparts".

## 행동·먹이(요약문)
- 위키 "take large insects, such as dragonflies", "Its speed and aerobatic skills enable it to take swallows and even swifts on the wing".
- NIBR "하늘에서 날개를 오므려 급강하하며 작은 새와 같은 먹이를 사냥한다."
